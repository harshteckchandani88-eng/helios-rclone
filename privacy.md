# Privacy policy

helios-rclone is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Google Drive solely to upload and verify the owner's own files in a folder the owner chose.

No data is collected, stored, sold or shared with anyone by this tool or its author.

Google sign-in tokens stay on the owner's own computer, in rclone's configuration file.

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
